🌙 A Portrait of My Beloved Tsuki 💖

My dearest Chisom, Merry Christmas, my love 🎄❤️ As this year draws to a close, I’ve found myself thinking about you more than ever, and I realized I can’t let this season pass without opening my heart to you fully. This is more than a Christmas greeting — it’s a confession that has lived quietly in me for most of my life. We’ve known each other for as long as I can remember, and somewhere along the way, without me even noticing when it began, I fell in love with you. Deeply. Completely. You became my Tsuki 🌙 — my moon — the light that pulled me out of some very dark nights just by being you. You didn’t even have to try. Your presence alone was enough. You are Ada, strong and beautiful in ways words struggle to capture. You are my Angel, not because you’re perfect, but because your sweetness, care, and heart constantly remind me how lucky I am. Even when you’re a handful, even when you nag (which you do a lot 😄), I know it’s your way of loving me, of wanting the best for us. And I love you for that. I won’t pretend I haven’t made mistakes. I almost lost you to my pride and my laziness, and that thought still humbles me. But if there’s one thing I’m sure of, it’s that my love for you is real and growing. I even tease you when you get jealous — not to hurt you, but because seeing it reminds me how deeply you care about me. I love everything about you: your voice, your smile, the way you smell, the way your mind works, your passion, your ambition, and your kindness. You support me in ways you may not even realize, and I carry that with me every day. Even though distance and school have kept us apart, every time we meet, there’s a fire between us that feels unstoppable ✨. Yet beyond the excitement, what I truly want is an eternity with you — slow or fast, loud or quiet. I want you in the good days and the bad ones, in happiness and sadness. Even when the excitement feels low, my love for you will never be. Being with you makes it hard to say goodbye. And loving you makes me never want to. Merry Christmas, my love. Thank you for being my moon, my angel, my Chisom ❤️
